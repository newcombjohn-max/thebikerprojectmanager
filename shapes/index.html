Field Guide Command Center

What the shapes mean

On a Command Center chart, and on a roadmap exported from it, the shape of a bar says what kind of row it is. Look at the ends of the bar.

Program

The theme bar with black lines through it at regular steps, so it stands out in any status color.

Project

A heavy rail with a cap that points up as well as down at each end.

Theme

A heavy rail with a big cap at each end: square at the top, straight down to a point, curving back up under the rail.

Objective

A medium rail with a ring at each end and a small point under it.

Summary task

A task with tasks under it: a light rail with a small pointed tab at each end.

Task

A solid bar. A milestone is a diamond.

Color says status, shape says kind. Gray is not started, green is on track, yellow is at risk, red is an issue and blue is complete, whatever the shape. A rail is shaded part of the way along to show how much of the work is done. A purple diamond is the go-live.

The shapes are shown here in gray. The same key is in the app: in Help, open the Visual guide, or on the Road Captain open Show and press Shape key.
